# Sustainable Forest Management Project

## Overview
This project aims to support sustainable forest management through the integration of modern technology and data-driven insights. By leveraging tools such as remote sensing, IoT, and AI-based analysis, the project helps monitor, protect, and enhance forest ecosystems.

## Objectives
- Promote responsible forest resource utilization.
- Monitor forest health and detect deforestation in real time.
- Calculate and visualize carbon sequestration potential.
- Support reforestation and conservation planning.

## Key Features
- **Carbon Sequestration Module:** Estimates carbon storage based on forest type, biomass density, and growth rate.
- **Remote Monitoring Tools:** Uses satellite and drone data to track forest cover and identify threats.
- **Data Dashboard:** Displays forest health indicators and sustainability metrics.
- **Community Involvement:** Encourages local participation and citizen reporting for sustainable forest stewardship.

## Contribution to Sustainable Forest Management
This project contributes to sustainable forest management by:
1. Providing actionable data to reduce illegal logging and deforestation.
2. Enhancing transparency and accountability in timber supply chains.
3. Supporting carbon accounting and climate change mitigation efforts.
4. Empowering policymakers, researchers, and local communities with reliable insights.
5. Encouraging sustainable practices that balance ecological, economic, and social needs.

## Future Plans
- Integrate real-time IoT sensors for soil and weather data.
- Expand carbon modeling to include region-specific emission factors.
- Develop an AI-powered forest fire risk predictor.
